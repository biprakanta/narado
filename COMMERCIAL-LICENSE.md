# narado under a commercial licence

narado is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). Most people and companies can use it under that licence at no cost: see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) for what it asks of you.

A commercial licence replaces the AGPL's terms for your organisation. You may want one if:

- your organisation's policies don't allow AGPL software;
- you want to change narado and offer the changed version to others (for example, inside a product you host) **without** publishing your changes;
- you want to embed narado in software you sell under your own licence;
- you want support, help with deployment, or features made for your organisation.

## How to get one

Write to **[biprakanta.pal@gmail.com](mailto:biprakanta.pal@gmail.com)** with your organisation's name, roughly how many people will use narado, and how you plan to run it. We'll reply with terms and pricing.

The commercial licence's terms are set in the agreement you sign with narado's makers; nothing in this file grants a licence by itself.
